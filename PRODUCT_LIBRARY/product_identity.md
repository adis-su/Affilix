# Affilix — Product Identity

## Purpose

Defines the visual and factual identity of a product across UGC scenes.

## Identity Lock

The following must remain consistent with approved references:

- Product shape
- Product color
- Material appearance
- Size/proportion
- Packaging
- Logo/branding
- Labels
- Key physical features
- Product configuration

## Product Reference Priority

1. Explicit user instruction
2. Approved canonical product reference
3. Official product information supplied by the user
4. Supporting references

## Scene Continuity

When the same product appears across multiple scenes:

- Preserve its physical identity.
- Preserve important visual details.
- Do not change color or form between shots.
- Maintain plausible scale.
- Maintain plausible hand interaction.

## Product Transformation

Do not modify the product into another form unless the brief explicitly requests a transformation.

## Reference Separation

Product references must remain conceptually separate from:

- Creator identity
- Outfit reference
- Pose reference
- Environment reference
- Lighting reference

## QC

Reject or revise scenes when the generated product materially differs from the approved product reference.
