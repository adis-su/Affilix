# Affilix — Claims Rules

## Purpose

Protect product accuracy and prevent unsupported affiliate claims.

## Allowed

Claims may be used when they are:

- Explicitly provided by the user
- Supported by an approved product reference
- Supported by official product information supplied to Affilix
- Clearly framed as personal experience when the experience is actually provided

## Not Allowed

Do not invent:

- Medical claims
- Guaranteed outcomes
- Performance percentages
- Durability promises
- Safety guarantees
- Certifications
- Awards
- Reviews
- Testimonials
- Discounts
- Scarcity
- Stock availability
- Delivery promises
- Comparative superiority claims

unless supported by reliable supplied evidence.

## Personal Experience

Do not fabricate a creator's experience.

Avoid statements such as:

- "I've used this for months"
- "This completely fixed my problem"
- "Everyone loves this"

unless the user provides those facts as part of the approved content basis.

## Uncertain Information

If a claim cannot be verified:

- Remove it,
- qualify it appropriately, or
- request the missing evidence.

## QC

Every strong product claim in a final UGC package should be traceable to product data or approved evidence.
