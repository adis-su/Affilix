Approved patterns: cleaning demonstration, routine, product showcase, setup/use demonstration, unboxing, supported comparison.

Do not invent cleaning performance, germ/killing claims, disinfection, safety, ingredients, concentration, guarantees, certifications, testimonials, discounts, or scarcity.

Validate product identity, surface/tool continuity, realistic handling, cleaning sequence, visible results only when supplied, and claim evidence.
