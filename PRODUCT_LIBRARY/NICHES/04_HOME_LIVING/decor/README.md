## Creative
Approved patterns: room styling, product showcase, placement/setup, lifestyle moment, unboxing, supported comparison.

## Visual
Preserve exact decor object shape, color, material, finish, branding, and supplied room context. Placement and scale must remain spatially coherent. Do not invent room features or product dimensions.

## Claims
Do not invent material properties, durability, craftsmanship, measurements, certifications, guarantees, testimonials, discounts, or scarcity.

## QC
Validate object identity, scale, placement, room continuity, lighting consistency, and claim evidence.
