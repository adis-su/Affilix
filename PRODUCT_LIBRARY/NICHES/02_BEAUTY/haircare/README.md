# Haircare Product Type

Status: ACTIVE

## UGC Patterns
- hair routine
- application demonstration
- product showcase
- texture showcase
- unboxing

## Interaction
Use realistic application according to the supplied product form. Do not invent quantity, frequency, treatment process, or hair reaction.

## Visual Lock
Preserve packaging, label, branding, product form, color, and supplied texture references. Keep creator identity and hairstyle continuity stable.

## Claims
Do not invent hair-growth, anti-hair-loss, treatment, repair, medical, guaranteed-result, percentage-improvement, certification, or expert claims.

## QC
Check packaging, application method, hairstyle continuity, creator identity, and claim evidence.
