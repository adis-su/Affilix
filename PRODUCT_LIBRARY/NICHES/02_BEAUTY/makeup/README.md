## Interaction
Application must match the supplied product type and realistic placement. Do not invent shade names, finish, wear duration, coverage, or performance.

## Visual Lock
Preserve packaging, applicator, product form, shade/color, branding, and supplied appearance references.

## Claims
Do not invent coverage, longevity, waterproofing, transfer resistance, skin benefits, certification, expert endorsement, or performance guarantees.

## QC
Check product packaging, applicator, shade consistency, application placement, creator identity, and unsupported claims.
