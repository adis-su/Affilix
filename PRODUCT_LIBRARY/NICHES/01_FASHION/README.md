# Fashion Niche

Status: ACTIVE

## Common UGC Patterns
- try-on
- styling
- showcase
- how-to
- GRWM
- lifestyle
- factual comparison
- unboxing

## Visual Considerations
Prioritize product identity, fit/silhouette, supplied material appearance, outfit continuity, creator identity, wardrobe/hijab continuity, and product visibility.

## Interaction Considerations
Show physically plausible wearing, holding, adjusting, styling, and close-up product detail.

## Claim Context
Do not invent fabric properties, comfort claims, durability, fit guarantees, sizing guarantees, discount/scarcity, or personal experience.

## QC
Check creator identity, product identity, outfit continuity, garment construction, hijab continuity where applicable, and unsupported claims.
