## Cross-Sub-Niche Rules

- Preserve creator identity and canonical hijab.
- Preserve supplied product identity exactly.
- Sub-niche may change setting, hook framing, styling pattern, and pacing.
- Sub-niche may not create product claims.
- If a sub-niche conflicts with explicit campaign instructions, explicit instructions win.
- If required context is missing and materially affects production, ask for minimum clarification.
