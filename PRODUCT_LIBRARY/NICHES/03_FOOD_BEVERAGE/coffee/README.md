# Food & Beverage — Coffee

Status: ACTIVE

## Identity
Preserve supplied coffee product name, packaging, roast/variant when supplied, beans/ground/powder form, preparation state, vessel, and supplied serving details.

## Creative
Approved patterns:
- coffee routine
- preparation demo
- product showcase
- pour demo
- lifestyle/work moment
- unboxing
- supported comparison

Do not fabricate taste, aroma, caffeine amount, roast characteristics, brewing performance, energy effects, or personal consumption history.

## Visual
- Preserve exact packaging, label, product form, vessel, and supplied preparation state.
- Brewing, pouring, stirring, and drinking must be physically plausible.
- Do not invent crema, steam, milk, sugar, ice, latte art, or other preparation details unless supplied.

## Claims
Allowed only from explicit campaign information, approved product data, supplied references, or explicitly supplied creator experience.

Do not invent caffeine amount, energy/focus effects, taste/aroma, roast profile, health benefits, freshness, certifications, awards, guarantees, testimonials, discounts, or scarcity.

## QC
Validate packaging, product form, preparation state, realistic brewing/handling, claim evidence, and continuity.
