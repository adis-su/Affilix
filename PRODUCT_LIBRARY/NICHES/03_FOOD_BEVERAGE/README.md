# Food & Beverage Niche

Status: ACTIVE

## Scope

Food & Beverage covers products intended for consumption, preparation, serving, or food/drink-related routines.

## Product Types

- snacks
- drinks
- coffee
- cooking

All listed product types currently have ACTIVE detailed rules.

## Creative Context

Common UGC patterns:
- product showcase
- unboxing
- preparation demo
- serving routine
- taste/texture demonstration only when supported by explicit creator experience or supplied evidence
- lifestyle consumption moment
- supported comparison
- cooking demonstration when recipe information is supplied

## Visual Context

Preserve supplied product identity, packaging, branding, variant, product form, serving/preparation state, and quantity where supplied.

Food and drink interactions must be physically plausible. Do not invent ingredients, garnish, ice, foam, steam, condensation, serving sizes, recipes, or preparation details.

## Claim Context

Allowed claim sources:
- explicit campaign information
- approved Product Library data
- supplied product references
- explicitly supplied creator experience

Do not invent:
- taste, aroma, texture
- ingredients or allergens
- nutritional values
- health benefits
- energy or performance effects
- freshness or shelf life
- certifications or awards
- guarantees
- reviews/testimonials
- discounts or scarcity

## QC Context

Validate:
- product identity and packaging
- variant/flavor when supplied
- food/drink state
- preparation/serving continuity
- realistic handling, pouring, eating, or drinking
- claim evidence
- creator experience claims
- downstream consistency

Food & Beverage rules are contextual and subordinate to the source-of-truth hierarchy.
