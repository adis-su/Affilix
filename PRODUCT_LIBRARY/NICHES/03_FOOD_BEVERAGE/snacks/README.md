# Food & Beverage — Snacks

Status: ACTIVE

## Identity
Preserve supplied product name, packaging, branding, flavor/variant, form, count/quantity, visible pieces, serving state, and any supplied preparation state.

## Creative
Approved patterns:
- snack showcase
- unboxing
- taste/texture demonstration
- serving idea
- lifestyle snack moment
- supported comparison
- routine/convenience context

Do not fabricate taste, texture, aroma, ingredients, nutritional values, dietary properties, health benefits, or personal consumption experience.

## Visual
- Keep packaging, label, variant, color, shape, and quantity consistent with supplied references.
- Food pieces must remain physically plausible.
- Eating, opening, pouring, serving, and handling should follow realistic motion.
- Do not invent garnish, preparation, serving size, or ingredients.

## Claims
Allowed only from explicit campaign information, approved product data, supplied references, or explicitly supplied creator experience.

Do not invent:
- health or nutritional benefits
- calories/macros
- ingredients/allergens
- taste or texture claims
- freshness/shelf-life
- certifications
- awards
- guarantees
- reviews/testimonials
- discounts/scarcity

## QC
Validate product identity, packaging/label, variant, food state, realistic handling/consumption, claim evidence, and continuity.
