# Food & Beverage Product-Type Schema

Every Food & Beverage product type should define:

## Identity
- product_type_id
- display_name
- status

## Creative
- ugc_patterns
- story_angles
- interaction_patterns

## Visual
- product_identity_fields
- preparation_or_consumption_visuals
- continuity_risks

## Claims
- allowed_claim_sources
- restricted_claims
- evidence_requirements

## QC
- identity_checks
- interaction_checks
- visual_checks
- claim_checks

Only explicitly defined fields are authoritative.
