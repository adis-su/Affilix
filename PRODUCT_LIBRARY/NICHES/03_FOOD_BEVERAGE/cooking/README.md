# Food & Beverage — Cooking Products

Status: ACTIVE

## Identity
Preserve supplied product name, packaging, product form, variant, quantity, included components, and supplied cooking/preparation state.

## Creative
Approved patterns:
- cooking demonstration
- recipe/application demo when recipe is supplied
- product showcase
- preparation workflow
- kitchen lifestyle
- unboxing
- supported comparison

Do not invent recipes, ingredients, measurements, cooking times, nutritional benefits, or personal experience.

## Visual
- Preserve exact packaging and product configuration.
- Cooking and handling must be physically plausible.
- Recipe steps must use only supplied or approved ingredients and quantities.
- Do not invent food ingredients or final-dish attributes.

## Claims
Allowed only from explicit campaign information, approved product data, supplied references, or explicitly supplied creator experience.

Do not invent nutritional/health benefits, ingredients/allergens, performance guarantees, cooking results, taste/texture, certifications, awards, testimonials, discounts, or scarcity.

## QC
Validate product identity, supplied recipe fidelity, preparation state, realistic handling, claims, and continuity.
