# Food & Beverage — Drinks

Status: ACTIVE

## Identity
Preserve supplied product name, packaging, branding, variant/flavor, liquid appearance, serving state, container, and supplied preparation details.

## Creative
Approved patterns:
- drink showcase
- pour/opening demonstration
- serving routine
- lifestyle moment
- preparation demo
- supported comparison
- unboxing

Do not fabricate taste, aroma, temperature, ingredients, nutritional values, health effects, or personal consumption experience.

## Visual
- Preserve exact container, label, cap/lid, variant, liquid appearance, and supplied serving state.
- Pouring and drinking must be physically plausible.
- Do not invent ice, garnish, straw, condensation, foam, or other serving details.

## Claims
Allowed only from explicit campaign information, approved product data, supplied references, or explicitly supplied creator experience.

Do not invent health or energy effects, nutritional values, ingredients, taste/aroma, temperature, freshness, certifications, awards, guarantees, testimonials, discounts, or scarcity.

## QC
Validate container/label, liquid state, serving state, realistic pouring/drinking, claim evidence, and continuity.
