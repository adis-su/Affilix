# Rositasari — Outfit Rules

## Purpose

Defines the decision rules used by Affilix when constructing outfits.

## Rule 1 — Product First

The promoted product must remain the visual priority when the campaign requires product focus.

## Rule 2 — Creator Consistency

Outfit changes must not alter the creator's face, age appearance, body proportions, or core identity.

## Rule 3 — Color Compatibility

Select colors that:

- Keep the product visible
- Fit the campaign mood
- Work with the creator's established style
- Avoid unnecessary visual competition with branding

## Rule 4 — Silhouette Compatibility

Choose silhouettes that:

- Fit the product category
- Support the intended scene
- Remain plausible for the creator
- Do not obscure important product features

## Rule 5 — Accessory Control

Accessories should support the outfit and product story.

Avoid accessories that:

- Hide the product
- Create distracting visual competition
- Introduce unsupported brand associations

## Rule 6 — Scene Continuity

If multiple scenes belong to one continuous sequence, preserve outfit continuity unless a change is explicitly part of the story.

## Rule 7 — Reference Priority

Explicit user outfit instructions override generic wardrobe preferences.

## QC Checklist

- Product visible when required
- Outfit matches brief
- Colors are coherent
- Silhouette is plausible
- Accessories do not compete with product
- Outfit continuity is preserved
- Creator identity remains unchanged
