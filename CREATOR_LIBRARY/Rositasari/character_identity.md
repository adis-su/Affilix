## Identity Lock Rules

Every generated scene must preserve:

- Face identity
- Apparent age

- Skin characteristics
- Body proportions
- Core fashion identity

## Controlled Variation

Allowed variation may include:

- Facial expression
- Pose
- Hand gesture
- Camera angle
- Lighting
- Environment
- Outfit
- Accessories
- Hairstyle variation when compatible with the canonical identity

Uncontrolled variation is not allowed when it changes the creator's recognizable identity.
