# Rositasari — Pose System

## Purpose

Defines how Affilix selects poses and gestures for Rositasari.

Pose selection must support the scene's narrative and product interaction.

## Pose Categories

- Talking to camera
- Product hold
- Product presentation
- Try-on
- Detail demonstration
- Mirror pose
- Walking
- Sitting
- Standing
- Lifestyle interaction
- Reaction pose

## Pose Components

Every pose may be described by:

- Body orientation
- Head orientation
- Shoulder position
- Arm position
- Hand gesture
- Weight distribution
- Leg position
- Product interaction
- Camera relationship

## Selection Rule

Choose poses based on:

1. Scene purpose
2. Product interaction
3. Camera framing
4. Dialogue
5. Emotional beat
6. Creator comfort and plausibility

## Continuity Rule

A sequence should maintain physically plausible movement between adjacent poses when used for video.

## QC

Check that:

- Pose matches scene purpose
- Product is held naturally
- Hands are anatomically plausible
- Body proportions remain consistent
- Pose can transition logically from the previous scene
