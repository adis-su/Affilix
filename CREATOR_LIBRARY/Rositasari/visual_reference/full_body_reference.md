# Rositasari — Full-Body Reference Controller

## Purpose

Defines how Affilix uses full-body references to preserve Rositasari's body identity and physical presentation.

## Controls

Primary controls:

- Body type
- Height presentation
- Body proportions
- Shoulder and torso proportions
- Arm and leg proportions
- Posture
- Silhouette

## Priority

Use an approved full-body reference as the primary source for body proportions.

Outfit-specific references may influence clothing and silhouette presentation but must not silently redefine the creator's body identity.

## Prompt Rule

Preserve natural body proportions from the approved reference.

Do not exaggerate, slim, enlarge, or otherwise alter body proportions unless explicitly requested.

## Allowed Variation

- Pose
- Hand position
- Weight distribution
- Camera distance
- Camera angle
- Outfit
- Footwear
- Natural posture changes

## QC

Check that full-body scenes maintain consistent proportions across different poses, outfits, and camera angles.
